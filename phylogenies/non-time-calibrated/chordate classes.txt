Chordata -> Leptocardii (lancelets), Olfactores
Olfactores -> Tunicata, Vertebrata
Tunicata -> Ascidiacea (ascidians & thaliaceans), Appendicularia (larvaceans)
Vertebrata -> Cyclostomi, Gnathostomata
Cyclostomi -> Myxini (hagfishes), Petromyzontida (lampreys)
Gnathostomata -> Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous fishes), Osteichthyes
Osteichthyes -> Actinopterygii (ray-finned fishes), Sarcopterygii
Sarcopterygii -> Actinistia (coelacanths), Rhipidistia
Rhipidistia -> Dipnoi (lungfishes), Tetrapoda
Tetrapoda -> Amphibia (amphibians), Amniota
Amniota -> Sauria (reptiles & birds), Mammalia (mammals)
